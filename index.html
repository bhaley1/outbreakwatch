Why a Directory Approach?

Direct links to authoritative sources provide the most reliable global outbreak intelligence

☣️ BSL-3/BSL-4 Monitoring
OutbreakWatch now actively monitors ProMED-mail for reports of BSL-3 and BSL-4 select agents. When high-containment pathogens are isolated, a prominent alert banner appears with details and maintains a permanent archive.
🍎 Global Foodborne Surveillance
Real-time monitoring of ECDC, PAHO, Health Canada, Food Standards Australia New Zealand, and other international authorities for foodborne illness outbreaks. Tracks global Salmonella, E. coli, Listeria, and Norovirus outbreaks with case counts, affected countries, and public health responses.
🔒 RSS Feed Limitations
ProMED-mail no longer offers free RSS feeds following their 2023 financial crisis. WHO Disease Outbreak News and HealthMap do not provide public API access. Most global surveillance systems have moved away from open RSS feeds.
🌍 Truly Global Coverage
This directory provides access to official sources spanning all continents — from WHO's international reports to PAHO's Americas coverage, ECDC's European surveillance, and WOAH's global animal health monitoring.
✅ Always Current
Direct links mean you always get the absolute latest data with no parsing delays, feed failures, or outdated caches. Each click takes you straight to the source's live dashboard.
📚 Educational Value
Rather than aggregating into a single feed, this approach introduces users to the full ecosystem of global disease surveillance — helping build data literacy across multiple authoritative systems.
