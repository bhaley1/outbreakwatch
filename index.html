🧪 Beta Tool — Preliminary Guidelines Only. This tool generates draft SOPs for orientation and training purposes. All outputs must be reviewed, adapted, and formally approved by a certified biosafety officer at your facility before use in any laboratory. Do not use as a substitute for institutional biosafety review.
⚠️ Important: Based on WHO Laboratory Biosafety Manual (4th ed., 2020) and BMBL (6th ed.). Always consult your institutional biosafety committee and comply with local regulations.
🚫 BSL-3 and BSL-4 are NOT covered by this tool. Work at BSL-3 (e.g. Mycobacterium tuberculosis, Brucella spp., West Nile virus, SARS-CoV-2 variants) and BSL-4 (e.g. Ebola, Marburg, Lassa, variola) requires specialized facility design, engineering controls, medical surveillance, regulatory authorization, and expert biosafety officer oversight that cannot be captured in a generic generated document. If you work at BSL-3 or BSL-4, contact your institutional biosafety committee directly for SOPs and do not use this tool.
